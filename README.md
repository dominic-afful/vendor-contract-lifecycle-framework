Vendor Contract Lifecycle Management Framework

Project overview

This independent portfolio project presents a practical framework for managing vendor contracts throughout their lifecycle.

It is designed to help procurement, finance, legal, IT, security and business teams improve contract visibility, accountability, risk management and renewal planning.

Objectives

* Standardise vendor contract intake and review.
* Improve contract approval and signature workflows.
* Establish supplier and contractual risk checkpoints.
* Track contract renewals and notice periods.
* Improve visibility of vendor commitments and expenditure.
* Support consistent contract documentation and audit trails.

Contract lifecycle

1. Business request and contract intake.
2. Vendor due diligence.
3. Commercial and contractual review.
4. Legal, security and privacy review where required.
5. Internal approvals.
6. Contract execution and repository filing.
7. Performance monitoring.
8. Renewal, amendment or termination.

Key stakeholders

Stakeholder	Responsibility
Procurement	Vendor coordination and commercial negotiations
Finance / FinOps	Budget, spend and financial approval
Legal	Legal review and contractual risk
IT	Technical assessment
Information Security	Security assessment
Privacy	Data protection review
Business Owner	Business need and performance oversight
